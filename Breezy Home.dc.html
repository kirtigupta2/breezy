HOW TO PLAY
1
Plug in the flute
Press PLAY, then CONNECT and pick the port. Only needed the first time — after that it finds itself.
2
Breathe out, twice
Every place starts by asking for two ordinary breaths. Not your biggest — the one that feels easy.
3
Play your way
The game measures those two breaths and sizes itself to you. A small pair of lungs and a big one both get a fair game.
4
No losing
Nothing runs out and no score goes down. A short breath just means your friend does not climb as high that time.
No flute yet?
Choose Spacebar on the same screen. Hold it to breathe out, let go to breathe in. Everything else works exactly the same.
